\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a Movie Recommendation Hub built as four Quarkus microservices with a Next.js frontend, Keycloak authentication, one PostgreSQL database per service and Kafka between the recommendation service and its worker. It runs locally with one command in Docker, and in Google Cloud on a self-hosted Kubernetes cluster created with Terraform and Ansible and monitored with Prometheus and Grafana. Recommendations come from Qwen2.5~3B served by Ollama, on demand and precomputed by a background agent.
